Source credits, licenses, and changes for the models included in Lumen’s browser experience.

Biology · Human heart

Kristen Browne; Heidi Schlehlein. 2022. 3D Reference Organ for Heart, Male v1.2. Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP. Based on Visible Human Male data from the National Library of Medicine.

Source and citation · CC BY 4.0

The original geometry and materials are bundled unchanged. Display scale, orientation, lighting, and temporary interaction highlights are adjusted in the viewer. Part labels match the source’s named meshes. This is an educational exploration specimen.

Computer hardware · MotherBoard + Components

Created by Daniel Cardona.

Original model on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0

Bundled from the existing Lumen desktop assets. Geometry, embedded textures, and authored materials are preserved. Display scale, orientation, lighting, and temporary interaction highlights are adjusted in the viewer. Only semantically identifiable authored materials receive component labels; other geometry remains visible and occludes selection.

Robotics · Industrial robot arm

Original simplified educational specimen created for Lumen. Its independently selectable base, shoulder, upper arm, elbow, forearm, and gripper illustrate the structure of a robot arm. It represents no specific commercial machine. Joint articulation is outside this release.

Interactive learning · Fruit study

Original simplified educational specimen created for Lumen. The apple, pear, and orange are individually selectable. Matching games are outside this release.

Hand tracking

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker by Google, distributed under Apache 2.0. The tracking model is bundled from the existing Lumen desktop assets. Model and WASM files are served locally; camera frames are processed on the visitor’s device.
